## Approved D015 scope

All four frozen D015 diagnostic pairs apply only to newly generated Gate-6
passes.  No D015 re-sweep of the completed Gate-5 corpus is authorized.

## Approved Gate-6 transition representation

Gate-6 may emit only the full label lattice, exact integer count tables, and
the complete list of face-adjacent unequal-label edges.  It may not fit,
smooth, rank, interpret, or otherwise infer a boundary.

## Approved cross-platform policy

Each Gate-6 pass must pin one platform fingerprint at start and refuse drift.
Gate-5 values may not be reused inside a V-14 artifact.  Overlap coordinates
must be recomputed and retained only as a bit-identity comparison artifact.

## Authorized Gate-5 identity registry

Creation of an additive per-shard Gate-5 digest registry is authorized.  It
must not modify the Gate-5 corpus.  The registry requires independent review
and a later owner ratification before it becomes a hash-pinned input to a full
Gate-6 pass.
